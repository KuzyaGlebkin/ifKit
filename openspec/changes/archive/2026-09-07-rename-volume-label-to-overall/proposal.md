## Why

In the Settings → Volume section, the label "Громкость" ("Volume" in English) is used for the overall volume control, which is ambiguous because the section name and the individual control share the same word. Renaming it to "Общая" ("Overall" in English) makes the control's purpose clear and improves UX.

## What Changes

- Rename the overall volume label from **"Громкость"** to **"Общая"** in Russian UI.
- Update the corresponding English localization string from **"Volume"** to **"Master"**.
- No functional or API changes; this is a pure UI label change.

### Modified Capabilities

- Settings screen UI labels and localization files (Russian and English).
- No impact on APIs, data models, or business logic.
